# Product

## Register

internal

## Platform

web

## Users

Sanggar Bundaku administration staff, dance studio lead (Sri Supeni), dance instructors, event coordinators, clients renting dance costumes or booking performances, and dance students/parents requiring official school permission letters (Surat Izin Dispensasi Sekolah).

## Product Purpose

Provide a streamlined, high-precision web application for Sanggar Bundaku to compose, edit, live-preview, and export official PDF invoices (Nota Sewa Kostum & Fee Penampilan) and bulk-personalized school permission letters (Surat Izin Dispensasi Sekolah) featuring official letterheads (Kop Surat), dancer watermarks, stamp placeholders, and signature sections.

## Positioning

Sanggar Bundaku Official Document Generator | Invoice & Surat Izin Dispensasi | Indonesian Traditional Dance Studio Administration Tool.

## Conversion & Proof

- **Primary CTAs**: "Export PDF", "Unduh PDF Surat Izin (Bulk)", "Cetak / Print Direct".
- **The line a user remembers after 10 seconds**: Sanggar Bundaku Document Generator: Effortlessly create and export official Indonesian dance studio invoices and bulk school permission letters with instant A4 live preview.
- **Belief ladder**:
  1. Sanggar administration can eliminate manual Word/Excel document formatting and errors.
  2. Official studio identity (Green Forest `#1F3F27`, Sandstone `#E2BD8B`, official logo & dancer watermark) is consistently maintained across all documents.
  3. Bulk generation of personalized school permission letters for dozens of students takes seconds instead of hours.
  4. Work-in-progress data is automatically saved locally, ensuring administrative work is resilient.
- **Proof on hand**: Real-time A4 print-ready preview with accurate Indonesian date & currency formatting (`Rp`), Kop Surat toggle, official signature block, and sample datasets for instant testing.

## Brand Personality

Professional, traditional Indonesian elegance, authoritative, reliable, clean, organized.

## Anti-references

Cluttered multi-page administrative software, generic unbranded invoice generators, slow manual layouting in Word processors, mismatched typography, and non-responsive mobile views.

## Design Principles

- **Indonesian Administrative Standard**: Exact compliance with official Kop Surat structure, Indonesian date formatting, currency display (`Rp`), itemized subtotaling, down payments, and formal closing signatures.
- **Live Dual-Pane Ergonomics**: Left panel dedicated to intuitive form inputs (5 columns), right panel providing a pixel-perfect, scaled A4 print preview (7 columns).
- **Brand Consistency**: Deep Forest Green (`#1F3F27`) and Sandstone (`#E2BD8B`) branding, subtle watermark overlay, and official logo placement.
- **Zero-Friction Bulk Exports**: Instant modal interface for selecting individual or all students to generate and download zipped/individual PDF files automatically.
- **Local Persistence & Recovery**: Automatic LocalStorage sync for drafts with quick reset to preset templates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Clear high-contrast dark UI shell (`slate-950`), WCAG AA compliant contrast for printed documents, keyboard navigable inputs, responsive mobile & tablet views.
